Bridging The Gap Between Agriculture and Urban Pantries

DKNK RETAILERS began with a clear purpose: eliminating unreasonable price markups and quality uncertainty in everyday staple staples. Traditional food grain and grocery chains frequently pass items through three or four intermediaries before they reach regional Kirana stores or individual kitchen shelves.

Headquartered in Delhi NCR, our dual-channel model serves two complementary sectors with equal passion: empowering everyday households with authentic, hygienic food grains at genuine competitive rates, while simultaneously supplying wholesale sacks (25kg/50kg) and master cartons to restaurants, cloud kitchens, hostel messes, caterers, and neighborhood retail stores.

By connecting directly with leading agricultural belts in Punjab, Haryana, Madhya Pradesh, and Rajasthan, our procurement team ensures fresh harvest rotation and direct quality oversight.

Privacy Policy

How DKNK RETAILERS collects, protects, and handles consumer and commercial client information.

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

At DKNK RETAILERS (referred to as "we", "our", or "us"), we operate both retail grocery delivery services and wholesale commodity supply operations across Delhi NCR. We recognize the importance of your personal and commercial data privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information gathered through our website, contact forms, WhatsApp communications, and order placement channels.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the necessary details required to fulfill delivery services, generate statutory tax invoices, and process commercial wholesale quotes:

  • Personal Contact Data: Name, delivery address, pin code, contact telephone/mobile number, and email address.
  • Commercial & Business Details: Business name, Trade Name, GSTIN number (for wholesale tax credit invoices), delivery dock instructions, and authorized receiving personnel details.
  • Order Communications & RFQs: Specific commodities requested, pack sizes (e.g. retail packets or 25kg/50kg bulk sacks), and dispatch preferences.

3. Purpose of Data Collection

The information we collect is strictly utilized for legitimate business operations:

  • Processing retail delivery dispatches to your doorstep within Delhi NCR.
  • Preparing volume quotes and executing wholesale consignment shipping.
  • Issuing legitimate GST tax invoices compliant with Government of India taxation laws.
  • Communicating dispatch status, order delays, or inventory restock updates via SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Ensuring accounting audits and legal FSSAI traceability compliance.

4. Web Analytics & Cookies

Our website utilizes standard web cookies and performance analytics services (including Google Analytics 4) to evaluate page traffic patterns, visitor interactions, and site responsiveness. These cookies collect anonymous telemetry, such as browser types, device resolutions, and pages visited, without harvesting personally identifiable sensitive payment records.

You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies at any time, though some functional conveniences of the website may be altered.

5. Non-Disclosure & Security Practices

We respect customer confidentiality. DKNK RETAILERS does not sell, trade, or rent your contact numbers or email lists to third-party telemarketers or external advertising platforms.

Information is only disclosed to:

  • Our dedicated logistics drivers and warehouse handlers for fulfilling delivery.
  • Taxation and statutory authorities where mandatory under applicable Indian law (e.g., GST filings).

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain transaction histories and billing records for the statutory retention periods prescribed under Indian financial and business laws. You have the right to request a copy of your stored commercial records or ask for the deletion of non-statutory contact preferences by writing to our data desk.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy, our privacy practices, or your data, please contact:
